## 建议

- Recall: always 12/12, risk_selective 9/12 (delta -3).
- Review calls: always 18, risk_selective 9 (saved 9); clean segments reviewed: always 6, selective 0.
- risk_selective missed serious errors in these annotation categories: mistranslation, omission. This reproduces the #20 finding that deterministic signals alone cannot see terminology drift, omission, negation and mistranslation; the measured misses are the cost of the skipped batches.
- Policy recommendation: keep the modes explicit rather than declaring them equivalent. If the default is risk_selective (#64), the report must not claim full-review recall, and skipped segments must stay visibly skipped (this run verifies reviewed=false, no approval, no TM promotion). A future mode that samples review work cannot be justified by this harness alone.
